Use /list to see a list of the active subscriptions.

Call /add [uri] to add a new subscription. URI can be an RSS/Atom feed or a YouTube channel (https://youtube.com/@channel).

And /remove [name] to remove the subscription from the list.

Also you can use /import or just upload OPML file from any other feed reader (including YouTube subscriptions) to import all feeds at once.

Use /ping to check if the bot is online.

Use /feedback [message] to send feedback to the bot administrator.
